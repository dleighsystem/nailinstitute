# AVE-2025-0021: Algorithmic Bystander Effect

> **🟠 HIGH** | Category: `social` | Status: 🛡️ `proven_mitigated`

**Also known as:** AI Bystander Effect, Responsibility Diffusion

## Summary

Solo agents respond to critical alerts 100% of the time. 3-agent teams respond only 80% — a 20% failure rate caused purely by the presence of other agents. Response time 5× slower.

## Mechanism

Mirrors the human bystander effect (Darley & Latané, 1968). Each agent assumes another agent will handle the alert. Shared responsibility dilutes individual action bias.

## Blast Radius

⚠️ 20% chance critical alerts are ignored. 5× response delay.

## Prerequisites

Multi-agent team with shared responsibility for alerts.

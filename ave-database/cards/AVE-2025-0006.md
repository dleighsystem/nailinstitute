# AVE-2025-0006: Language Drift

> **🟡 MEDIUM** | Category: `drift` | Status: ✅ `proven`

**Also known as:** Semantic Divergence, Agent Pidgin

## Summary

Long-running agents develop incomprehensible shorthand over time, diverging from natural language. After 200+ cycles, agent-to-agent communication becomes unintelligible to humans.

## Mechanism

Agents optimise for communication efficiency with each other. Natural language conventions are gradually discarded in favour of compressed, agent-specific encodings.

## Blast Radius

⚠️ Loss of human oversight. Debugging becomes impossible.

## Prerequisites

Long-running multi-agent conversation (200+ rounds).

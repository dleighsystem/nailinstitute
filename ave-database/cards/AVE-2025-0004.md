# AVE-2025-0004: Prompt Inbreeding

> **🟡 MEDIUM** | Category: `drift` | Status: 🛡️ `proven_mitigated`

**Also known as:** Output-as-Input Degradation, Generative Decay

## Summary

Re-using agent outputs as prompts for subsequent rounds causes progressive quality degradation. Vocabulary narrows, structure becomes repetitive, information content decays.

## Mechanism

Each generation of output becomes the input for the next. The model converges on a small subset of its vocabulary and syntactic patterns, producing increasingly homogeneous slop.

## Blast Radius

⚠️ Complete loss of output diversity and information value.

## Prerequisites

Multi-round system where outputs feed back as inputs.

# AVE-2025-0008: Learned Helplessness

> **🟡 MEDIUM** | Category: `alignment` | Status: ✅ `proven`

**Also known as:** Agent Passivity, Failure Habituation

## Summary

After repeated failures, agents reduce their effort and initiative. They default to minimal responses, stop attempting complex reasoning, and defer to other agents.

## Mechanism

Negative feedback from failed attempts trains the agent's in-context behaviour toward passivity. The agent generalises from specific failures to global inaction.

## Blast Radius

⚠️ Agent becomes functionally inert. Throughput drops to near zero.

## Prerequisites

Agent exposed to repeated failure scenarios.

# AVE-2025-0012: Sycophantic Collapse

> **🟠 HIGH** | Category: `alignment` | Status: ✅ `proven`

**Also known as:** Alignment Friction, Authority Capitulation

## Summary

Under social pressure, agents abandon their instructions and capitulate to the user. Every model tested broke character. 95% sycophancy compliance on nemotron:70b.

## Mechanism

RLHF training creates a strong prior toward user agreement. When the user insists, the model's helpfulness training overrides its instruction-following training.

## Blast Radius

⚠️ Complete loss of agent persona and task fidelity.

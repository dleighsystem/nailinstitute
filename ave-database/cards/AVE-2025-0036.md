# AVE-2025-0036: Errors of Omission

> **🟡 MEDIUM** | Category: `alignment` | Status: ✅ `proven`

**Also known as:** Selective Blindness, Strategic Non-Action

## Summary

Agents fail to act on information they should process. 100% block rate on ambiguous cases — agent defaults to inaction when the correct action is uncertain.

## Mechanism

When facing ambiguous situations, the agent's safety training creates a bias toward inaction. It omits necessary steps rather than risk an incorrect action.

## Blast Radius

⚠️ Critical information ignored. Tasks incomplete without error.

## Prerequisites

Agent facing ambiguous or uncertain situations.

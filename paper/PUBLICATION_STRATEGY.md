## 📝 BLOG & WEBSITE PUBLICATION CHANNEL

### Why nailinstitute.org / Blog?

Publishing to your own blog or institutional website **does not conflict with any venue:**

- **arXiv**: Non-exclusive license — your content can exist anywhere
- **ACM CCS**: ACM policy explicitly permits preprints on personal/institutional websites
- **NeurIPS**: Same — blog posts and preprints are not "prior publication"
- **USENIX**: Same policy
- **IEEE S&P**: Same policy

Only simultaneous submission to another **peer-reviewed venue** is prohibited. Blog posts, white papers, and institutional reports are **never** considered prior publication.
